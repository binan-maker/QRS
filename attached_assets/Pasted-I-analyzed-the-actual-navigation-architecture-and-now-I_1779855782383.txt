I analyzed the actual navigation architecture and now I can clearly explain WHY your bottom navigation feels delayed when switching between:

* Home
* Generator
* History
* Profile

This is NOT mainly an animation issue.

The delay is caused by:

# Heavy screen mounting + expensive hooks + Firestore calls + oversized contexts + rerender chains

Your architecture is advanced, but currently the navigation system is carrying too much work during tab switches.

Your tabs use:


# GOOD
