# Major Performance Issue #4

QR Regeneration

Potential issue:

Every input change:

```tsx
setInputValue()
```

causes:

```text
whole generator rerender
whole preview rerender
QRCode rerender
```

The QRCode component is expensive.

Current likely flow:

```text
Type
↓
Generate new QR
↓
Rerender SVG
```

for every keystroke.

Not ideal.

---

# Better

Use:

```tsx
useDeferredValue()
```

or

```tsx
debounce(300ms)
```

before regenerating.

# Final Verdict

The QR Generator is **more complex and heavier than the Home Page**, but it is also one of the strongest engineered parts of the app. The biggest problems are not bugs—they are **growing complexity, duplicate landing components, oversized components (especially `QrOutputCard`), too many mounted modals, and unnecessary rerenders during typing**. Those are the areas most likely to improve responsiveness and maintainability.
