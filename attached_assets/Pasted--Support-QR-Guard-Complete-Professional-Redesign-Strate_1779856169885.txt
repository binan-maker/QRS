Your current “Support QR Guard” section/page has a few major UX problems:

* Too much text
* Large oversized buttons
* Heavy purple everywhere
* Emotional overload
* Weak visual hierarchy
* Looks more like a temporary support screen
* Not enough premium/trust feeling
* Donation request appears too aggressive
