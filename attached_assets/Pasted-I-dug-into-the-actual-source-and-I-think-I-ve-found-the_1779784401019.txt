I dug into the actual source, and I think I've found the root architectural problem.

## Current Situation

Your app has **three separate QR data worlds**:

### 1. Generated QR Codes

Saved here:

```text
users/{userId}/generatedQrs
```

via:

```ts
saveGeneratedQr()
```

Contains:

```ts
content
contentType
templateKey
displayDestination
formValues
qrType
uuid
```

---

### 2. Scan History

History screen uses:

```text
features/history/
```

and loads scan records through:

```ts
getUserScansPaginated()
```

This is a completely different collection.

History is showing:

```text
QRs scanned by user
```

NOT:

```text
QRs generated by user
```

---

### 3. QR Detail

QR Detail loads:

```ts
useQrDetail(id)
```

which reads from:

```text
qrCodes
```

and guard links.

Not from:

```text
users/{uid}/generatedQrs
```

directly.

---

# Why Your Generated Type Is Missing

Suppose a user creates:

```text
WiFi QR
```

Generator saves:

```ts
contentType = "wifi"
templateKey = "wifi"
formValues = {...}
```

inside:

```text
users/{uid}/generatedQrs
```

Later:

### My QR

Loads generatedQrs.

Works mostly.

### History

Loads scans collection.

Never sees generatedQrs.

Therefore:

```text
WiFi type disappears
```

---

### QR Detail

Loads:

```text
qrCodes/{qrId}
```

Many generated fields are never copied there.

So:

```text
templateKey
formValues
displayDestination
```

may be missing.

Result:

```text
QR Detail falls back to text/url
```

instead of:

```text
WiFi
Event
UPI
Location
vCard
Business
```

---

# The Real Design Problem

Right now:

```text
Generator
      ↓
generatedQrs

Scanner
      ↓
scans

QR Detail
      ↓
qrCodes
```

Three different sources.

That's why behavior becomes inconsistent.

---

# What You Should Do

Create ONE central QR model.

Example:

```ts
QrRecord
{
  id
  ownerId

  content
  contentType
  templateKey

  formValues

  displayDestination

  qrType

  createdAt

  scanCount

  isActive
}
```

Store this in:

```text
qrCodes/{qrId}
```

as the master record.

---

Then:

### Generator

Writes:

```text
qrCodes
```

### My QR

Reads:

```text
qrCodes
```

### QR Detail

Reads:

```text
qrCodes
```

### Analytics

Reads:

```text
qrCodes
```

### History

References:

```text
qrCodes
```

---

# Architecture I Recommend

```text
qrCodes
│
├── qrId
│   ├── content
│   ├── contentType
│   ├── templateKey
│   ├── formValues
│   ├── displayDestination
│   ├── qrType
│   ├── scanCount
│   └── ownerId
│
├── qrId
│
└── qrId
```

Then:

```text
generatedQrs
```

becomes only:

```text
user shortcuts
```

or can be removed entirely.

---

# Most Likely Immediate Bug

Looking at `saveGeneratedQr()`:

It writes:

```ts
templateKey
displayDestination
formValues
```

to:

```text
users/{uid}/generatedQrs
```

but the corresponding write to:

```text
qrCodes/{qrId}
```

only stores:

```ts
content
contentType
ownerId
ownerName
qrType
businessName
```

I do **not** see:

```ts
templateKey
formValues
displayDestination
```

being copied into the main QR record.

That means:

```text
Generator knows WiFi
↓
Saved correctly
↓
qrCodes loses WiFi metadata
↓
QR Detail only sees generic content
```

This is the first thing I would fix.

### In short

The issue is not one screen. The issue is that QR metadata is being saved in one collection (`generatedQrs`) but detail/history pages are reading from other collections (`qrCodes`, scans). Centralizing around a single master QR record in `qrCodes` will make the type, template, and structured data appear consistently across My QR, QR Detail, Analytics, and History.
