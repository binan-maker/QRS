I dug into the QR Details architecture. This page is actually one of the most complex parts of your entire app.

# How QR Details Works

When user scans a QR:

```text
Scanner
   ↓
QR Result
   ↓
app/qr-detail/[id].tsx
   ↓
QrDetailScreen.tsx
```

Then the router decides:

```text
Static QR
Guard QR
Standard QR
```

using:

```tsx
if (guardUuid)
   GuardQrDetailScreen

if (standardUuid)
   StandardQrDetailScreen

else
   StaticQrDetailScreen
```

So you currently have **3 different QR Detail systems**.
