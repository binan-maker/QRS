🔐 Security & Trust Considerations
Since QR Guard is a cybersecurity app, your follow system must prevent abuse:
Risk
Mitigation
Fake creators spamming followers
• Require phone/UPI verification for creator status
• Rate-limit follow requests
• Community reporting tools
Malicious QRs gaining followers
• Auto-unfollow if QR is flagged as phishing
• Notify followers when followed QR is deactivated
Privacy leaks via follower lists
• Make follower lists private by default
• Allow creators to hide follower count
Bot networks inflating metrics
• CAPTCHA for mass-follow attempts
• Analyze follow patterns for suspicious activity
