Based on the project structure you uploaded and the scanner-related modules I inspected earlier, the **Scanner feature is the most performance-critical screen in your entire app** because it continuously uses the camera, QR detection engine, animations, navigation, permissions, and parsing logic simultaneously.

```text

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
Barcode Scanner
    ↓
QR Detection
    ↓
Parser
    ↓
Navigation
    ↓
QR Detail Screen
```

This is the heart of the entire application.

# Biggest Performance Risk

## Camera Re-Renders

If your scanner screen contains:

```tsx
const [qrData, setQrData] = useState(...)
```

and QR updates trigger:

```tsx
setQrData(...)
```

frequently,

then:

```text
Camera
↓
Whole Scanner Screen
↓
Whole Overlay
↓
Bottom Components
```

can rerender.

This is one of the biggest causes of lag in scanners.

---

# What Should Happen

Only:

```text
Camera
↓
Detection Callback
```

should run.

The entire screen should NOT rerender every detection.
